# Advice or Advise?

Both spellings are correct, but they have slightly different meanings. Even in our own documentation and other official documentation like the HBO-I , we sometimes use "S" and sometimes "C," as shown in Figure 1 of the IT-DomainDescription HBO-I.

According to Dictionary.com, "Advice" is a noun that means guidance on what someone should do. Advise is a verb that offers guidance to someone. In other words, to advise someone is to give them advice.
