## Administrator: Keanu Pham
![Keanu](https://tse1.mm.bing.net/th?id=OIP.pFqcVa-wLxxMp2ytf2ZhzgHaFY&pid=Api)
<br/><br/>
Keanu Pham is a 32 year old software developer, specialised in Web Development. During high school, Keanu noticed that programming was a natural talent of his and after writing his A-levels, he directly started to study, first a Bachelor in Informatics and then a Master which specialised him in Web Development. Since 3 years now, he is working at Appcomm after changing jobs once.
Keanu is really driven and always wants to finish what he starts, usually perfectly. He wants to know all the information before making any kind of decision. But exactly that is one of his problems. He has issues with quick answers, having a good overview of at example his customers and always has to extensively look at different sources to find the relevant information.
Keanu wants to be thorough, so for him it is important that the system he uses gives him the option to view a lot of information at once, and he also directly wants to interact with the customer. At example, if he gets any kind of message, request or ticket from a customer, he wants to be able to see all information, base his answer on that and respond to the correct person for the issue. Furthermore, Keanu is a big fan of having everything at the same space, since this makes the whole process way more structured; and he likes structure, a lot.

Andreas Gerlinger is a 52-year old entrepreneur from Germany. He is founder of a medium-sized marketing company, specialised on smaller companies near the boarder. Before he founded the company, he moved to his wife in the Netherlands, and founded the company in the vicinity of Venlo. His interest is purely from the marketing view; he doesn't know and doesn't really care about fancy software, he just wants to get the job done. And specifically in the last years, he was forced to get more and more digital, even though he doesn't feel as comfortable in this area. For him, using a website would have two main reasons: Repetitively looking at the same overview of his company ,with slight changes, but the overall structure of the information shouldn't change, and second to contact AppComm if there is an issue. TIme is not as important in that regard: If he doesn't call, it's not too urgent, that's his motto.

## Customer 2: Marlene van den Boom
![Marlene](https://tse4.mm.bing.net/th?id=OIP.RT1QnSr-D7-P2IXGhty-6AHaFA&pid=Api)
<br/><br/>
Marlene van den Boom, 40 years old, is responsible for managing different projects at the company she works for. All of those projects belong to the same product, and there are different companies they work with to improve and change the product. In the case of AppComm, Marlene has to manage and have an overview over various services that were and are provided by AppComm, and she is also the contact person for her employees if there is an issue. Until now, she had to write a mail or call for every small issue or question, but that is quite annoying for her. She doesn't like to be disrupted at work, so for her it would be really convenient if the answers from AppComm would be visible to more people than just her, since she doesn't get any notifications so she sees mails and calls late. She is still quite new at the company and needs to get adjusted regarding her type of working, but  for the time being this is the way she works, and the people who need to have an issue fixed or a question answered have to live with this, which is why it would be very handy if a second or third person would have access to the communications tool and more importantly, if those people could be directly contacted from AppComm due to their work area and due to the fact that they are present as a contact person in AppComm's application.
