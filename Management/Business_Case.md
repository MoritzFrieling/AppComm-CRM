## Contribution to Business Strategy:

The project makes it more easy to manage customers data. Due to the different data streams and sources, the projects main aim is to combine the different sources into one CRM (Customer Relationship Management) tool, specifically all their general information, needed to contact and communicate, but also all the developed products and services that the customer uses.

## Options Considered:
- Software directly accessing the customers internal database (specifically, their „Robot“ used to organize data)
- Software using a database for setup, then decoupeling the head (database) -> (selected)


## Benefits:
- Complete overview over customers data, including general information but also their products and services.
- Customers are more satisfied due to having all the information at one place and quickly

## Risks:
- Quality: Risk of the project not meeting the customers (AppComm’s) needs. For this reason a close involvement of the customer and good communication is required.
